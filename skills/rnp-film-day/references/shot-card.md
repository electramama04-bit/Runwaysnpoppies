# FILM DAY — SHOT CARD

Screenshot this. Six shots. Keep it on your lock screen.

---

**FIRST, IN THE PARKING LOT**

**1. THE HOOK — 3 takes, 10 sec each**
Say the line. Three times. Don't over-coach.
Out of the wind. Phone two feet from the mouth.
→ Monday POV

---

**WALKING IN**

**2. ARRIVAL REACTION — 15 sec**
Camera on their FACES, not the building.
Walk behind them, shoot forward.
→ Sunday cold open

---

**WHENEVER IT HAPPENS**

**3. THE MOMENT — 30 to 60 sec**
The quiet. The question. The correction.
**FILM THE SECOND IT STARTS.** Don't wait to see if it becomes something.
Missed it? Tell it to camera in the car. Never restage.
→ Sunday payoff — THIS IS THE ONE THAT MATTERS

---

**SOMETIME MID-TRIP**

**4. THE NUMBER SHOT — 20 sec**
Film the proof. Signs, receipts, odometer, a count on camera.
→ Wednesday outcome

**5. KID EXPLAINING — 30 sec**
One question: "So how does that actually work?"
Let him be 80 percent right.
→ Friday contrarian

---

**LAST, WHEN EVERYBODY IS TIRED**

**6. B-ROLL — 6 clips, 5 to 8 sec, held still**
Wings. Tools. Hands. A taxi. A prop. The walk back.
**Never the first shot of a post.**
→ Seconds 3 to 15 of all four
