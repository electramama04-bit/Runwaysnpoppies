## About page

249 words, against a 250 word limit.

```
My son doesn't fit in a square. Neither did I.

Public school put blue tape on the floor around his desk so he would stay inside
it. He was 6. I pulled him out and built him a runway instead.

Every subject in his curriculum runs through aviation, because that is what pulls
his attention forward. History is the Wright Brothers and the pilots who flew in
the war. Math is flight physics, altitude, and drag. Science is weather and
aerodynamics. Reading is pilot memoirs and the letters veterans wrote home.

I am a Boeing tool maker. For sixteen years I have made the parts that keep
aircraft in the air. I write our school year at the kitchen table before my shift.

I am also Chaplain of VFW Auxiliary Post 969 in Tacoma, and the daughter of an Air
Force veteran of foreign wars. My dad is the Quartermaster at our Post. That is
where the poppies come from.

Runways for my son. Poppies for my dad.

Here is what you get. One letter a week. What we built, what worked, what did not,
and the printable that came out of it. Lesson pages, lab sheets, trip cards, and
the hours log I use to stay right with the state of Washington.

Three of those files are free on the site right now, no email at all, if you would
rather start there. runwaysnpoppies.com

If you wear more than one hat too, you are in the right place.
```
