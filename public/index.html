The Aviation Homeschool Approach

My son doesn't fit in a square. Neither did I.

When public school put blue tape around his desk to keep him inside it, I pulled him out and built him a runway instead. Every subject in his curriculum runs through aviation — because that's what pulls his attention forward.

History is the Wright Brothers, WWII pilots, my dad's Air Force service, the postwar aerospace boom that built Boeing. Math is flight physics, altitude, fuel calculations, drag. Science is weather, aerodynamics, the reason nuclear cooling towers exist (hello, Satsop). Reading is pilot memoirs, aviation history, and the letters veterans wrote home.

The world is our classroom — especially the parts with wings.
