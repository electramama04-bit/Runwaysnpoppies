# Location Notes — what each place actually allows

**Nothing in the Rules column is confirmed yet.** This file starts empty on purpose. Fill a
row the day you are there, at the desk, from a person who works there. Then it is real.

Locations are pulled from the 2026-2027 trip list in **aviation-homeschool**. Add rows for
anywhere not listed.

## How to fill a row

At the desk, ask three questions and write down the answers:

1. "Can I record video on my phone here?"
2. "Is a tripod or a gimbal okay, or handheld only?"
3. "Anywhere in the building that's off limits for filming?"

Then after the visit, add what you learned about **wind and noise**, and the **one thing
worth filming** so the next trip starts ahead.

## Status key

- **Confirmed** — asked a staff member on site, wrote down what they said
- **Unconfirmed** — never asked. Ask before you set anything up
- **Visited** — trip already happened, notes are from memory, verify next visit

## The Boeing rule — treat this as settled

Nothing filmed inside a Boeing facility goes on camera without explicit written clearance
from Boeing. Mama being an employee makes this stricter, not looser. The factory tour is
widely reported to bar personal items including phones from the tour floor — confirm when
booking, but assume no.

"Nobody stopped me" is not clearance.

What this leaves, and it is plenty: film outside the fence, film the tools at home, film
the drive, film the boys talking about what they saw. The Boeing credibility in a Runways
n Poppies post comes from what Mama knows, not from footage of where she knows it.

## Airshows and active airfields

- Ear protection before camera, every time. Both boys, no exceptions.
- Spoken lines are impossible on the flight line. Film them in the car on the way in.
- Ramp, tarmac, and movement areas are escorted access. Ask the operator or the FBO, and
  ask before the day, not during.
- Military installations set their own rules and they are not the museum's rules. Ask early.

## Art museums vs air museums

Air museums tend to be relaxed about handheld phone video. Art museums often are not,
because of loan agreements on individual works they do not control. Six of this year's
stops are art museums or have an art wing. Assume no until a person says yes.
