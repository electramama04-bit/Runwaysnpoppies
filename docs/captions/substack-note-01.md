## How to post it

Paste the text, then paste the link on its own line underneath. Substack turns
it into a preview card with the title and image, so do not describe the post in
the note as well. Let the card do that.

## Why A

Nine words before the reader knows something is wrong. "He was 6" lands alone.
The post title is already the same story, so the card underneath finishes the
thought instead of repeating it.
