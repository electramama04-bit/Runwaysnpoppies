## The filming safety net

For every heavy post (Weeks 2 and 3), in this order:

1. **Film the on-camera version first.** Try it once, all the way through. If you cry,
   keep going. Do not restart. Do not wipe it away. Real is the entire point.
2. **The rule: one honest tear, once.** Then cut to Noah on the runway. The pivot from
   pain to redemption is what makes it a story instead of a spectacle. There is a whole
   2026 trend of creators farming tears for views. You are not that, and the way you stay
   not-that is the hard cut at second 26.
3. **Have the text-overlay fallback ready** (Scripts 3-ALT and 5-ALT). If you watch the
   on-camera version Wednesday morning and it doesn't feel right, you post the fallback.
   You are never stuck, and you never skip a drop.
4. **Show Noah both versions before either one posts.** He gets a veto. No explanation
   required from him, no negotiating from you.
5. **Have the reply bank open** (`comment-replies.md`) before you post, not after.

## Kids-on-camera rules

- Noah appears on camera when he **wants** to. His face is in the **redemption** half only.
- On Blue Tape and Finger-Guns: hands, back of head, silhouette, or present-day happy
  footage only.
- **Never re-enact the trauma.** No blue tape props. No staged classroom. Ever.
- When he is on camera, have him look **at the lens**, not off to the side.
- Build the channel so it works with or without him on screen. Your voice, your story,
  your Boeing lens carries it. He is a bonus, not the engine — because in five years he
  may want off the internet, and he gets to have that.

## Posting mechanics

- **Face + text overlay together, every time.** 85% of Reels are watched on mute. If you
  speak the hook without putting it on screen, the hook does not exist.
- Hook line on screen in the first 3 seconds. Top third of the frame, above the caption bar.
- Vertical 9:16. Shoot in landscape only for the wide Satsop tower shots, then crop.
- Facebook long-forms: first two lines carry it — everything after "See more" is for the
  people you already have.
