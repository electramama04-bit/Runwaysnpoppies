# The Eight Hook Formulas

Retention figures come from OpusClip's analysis of 1.9 million short-form clips, reported
in Instapdown's 2026 travel-hook analysis and CreatorHouse's February 2026 travel Reel
dataset. Full links in `sources.md`.

## The rule everything else hangs on

**The hook has to manufacture tension inside the first 1.5 seconds.** Beautiful footage
of a plane does not stop the thumb. A number, a challenge, or an unexpected reveal does.
Save the beautiful footage for seconds 3 to 15, after the viewer has already committed.

## The eight

| # | Formula | Retention | Wins when | Aviation version |
|---|---------|----------:|-----------|------------------|
| 1 | **Specific Outcome (numbers)** | 45% | Any Reel under 30 sec | "We flew 3 airports, ate at 2 diners, and Noah logged his first taxi clearance in one Saturday." |
| 2 | **POV** | 42% | Less-saturated niches, which is yours | "POV: your mom is a Boeing toolmaker and Saturday school is at the flight museum." |
| 3 | **Contrarian / Unpopular Opinion** | 38% | Saturated niches | "Museum tours are the worst way to teach kids about airplanes. Here's what we do instead." |
| 4 | **Curiosity Gap (tease and reveal)** | Strong 3-sec hold | 15 to 30 sec | "The reason my kid understands lift better than most adults isn't textbooks. It's this $12 thing." |
| 5 | **Direct Address + Stake** | High | 30+ sec | "If you're taking your kids to the Museum of Flight this weekend, do NOT skip the second floor." |
| 6 | **Confessional ("I wish I knew")** | High | Talking-head Reels | "I wish someone told me you don't need a curriculum to raise a kid who loves aviation." |
| 7 | **Number That Demands Explanation** | Very high | Under 15 sec | "5 aviation field trips within 90 minutes of Seattle. Free." |
| 8 | **Specific-Scene Cold Open** | Very high | 30+ sec | "We're on the tarmac at Paine Field and Ethan just corrected the pilot's flight plan." |

## How to choose

- **Under 15 seconds?** Formula 7 or 1. A bare number does the whole job.
- **15 to 30 seconds?** Formula 4 or 2. You need an open loop to survive the middle.
- **Over 30 seconds?** Formula 8 or 5. A scene or a stake buys you the runway.
- **Talking to camera in the car?** Formula 6. Confession only works on a face.
- **Saying something the homeschool world disagrees with?** Formula 3. Highest ceiling,
  highest risk. Use it on Fridays where it belongs.

## What not to do

Four rules, each earned from the data.

1. **Do not lead with pretty sky or plane footage.** Scenery loses to conflict, surprise,
   and numbers. B-roll belongs at seconds 3 to 15.
2. **Do not say "come learn with us" or "let's explore."** These are the exact phrases the
   algorithm has been trained to skip. They read as classroom marketing. Say what
   happened *today* instead.
3. **Do not be vague with numbers.** "A few airports" gets scrolled past. "3 airports in
   4 hours" does not. Specificity is the whole game.
4. **Do not front-load the aerospace curriculum.** Homeschool credibility shows up at
   seconds 5 to 15, not seconds 0 to 2. Fun first, lesson second, every time.

## Cross-check against the general hook library

The **viral-hooks** skill holds a 100-hook framework library across 13 categories and the
first-3-words test. Run any hook from this file through that test before filming: read
the first three words alone and ask whether they create curiosity, surprise, or pull.

- "Here's what I" fails. No pull.
- "We flew 3" passes. Number plus open loop.
- "Museum tours are" passes. Sets up a fight.

Cut filler off the front. The strongest word goes first.
