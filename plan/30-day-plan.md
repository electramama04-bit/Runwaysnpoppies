### Why the name works, and why it changes the plan

Runways and poppies are not two separate things she happened to like. **They met in the same
field.** The poppies of "In Flanders Fields" grew over ground where the first military
airfields were cut — grass strips, canvas machines, 1915. Aviation and remembrance were born
in the same mud, about a mile apart.

That is the channel's whole thesis in one image, and it's why this plan now has **four**
buckets instead of three.

## The filming safety net

For every heavy post (Blue Tape, Finger-Guns), in this order:

1. **Film the on-camera version first.** All the way through, once. If you cry, keep going.
   Do not restart. Do not wipe it away. Real is the entire point.
2. **One honest tear, once.** Then cut to Noah. The pivot from pain to redemption is what
   makes it a story instead of a spectacle.
3. **The fallback is not a lesser version.** If you watch it Wednesday morning and it isn't
   right, post 3-ALT or 5-ALT. You are never stuck, and you never skip a drop.
4. **Show Noah both versions before either posts.** He gets a veto. No reason required from
   him, no negotiating from you.
5. **Have the reply bank open before you post, not after.**

## Kids-on-camera rules

- Noah appears when he **wants** to. His face is in the **redemption** half only.
- Blue Tape and Finger-Guns: hands, back of head, silhouette, or present-day happy footage.
- **Never re-enact the trauma.** No blue tape props. No staged classroom. Ever.
- When he's on camera, have him look **at the lens**, not off to the side.
- Build the channel so it works with or without him. Your voice, your Boeing lens, your
  chaplain's eye carry it. In five years he may want off the internet, and he gets to have that.

## Posting mechanics

- **Face + text overlay together, every time.** 85% of Reels are watched on mute. A spoken
  hook with no text on screen is a hook that doesn't exist.
- Hook line on screen in the first 3 seconds, top third, above the caption bar.
- Vertical 9:16. Shoot landscape only for the wide Satsop tower shots, then crop.
- Facebook long-forms: the first two lines are the whole job — everything past "See more"
  is for people who already stayed.
