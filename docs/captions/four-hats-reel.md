# Four Hats Reel — captions, all platforms

The pinned introduction Reel. 30 seconds, 9:16, text overlay over music, no spoken audio.

Written to the standing voice rules in `skills/rnp-reel-hooks/SKILL.md`:
no em dashes, no emojis in the body, short sentences, first person, present
tense, and every caption ends pointing at the free library rather than a vague
"follow along."

**Publish order:** YouTube first, because it holds the full description. Then
share the YouTube link to Facebook. Instagram, TikTok, and Pinterest get their
own native upload with the short captions below.

### Description

```
One woman. Four hats.

Boeing tool maker. Homeschool mom. VFW Auxiliary Chaplain. Veteran's daughter.

I make aircraft parts for a living. I come home and teach my son through
aviation. I serve the military families of VFW Post 969 in Tacoma. And I am the
daughter of an Air Force veteran of foreign wars.

Runways for my son. Poppies for my dad.

That is where the name comes from.

The runway is the one I built for my son. Public school put blue tape on the
floor around his desk, a square, so he would stay inside it. He was 6. I pulled
him out and built him a curriculum around the thing that pulls his attention
forward. History is the Wright Brothers and the pilots who flew in the war. Math
is flight physics, altitude, and drag. Science is weather and aerodynamics.
Reading is pilot memoirs and the letters veterans wrote home.

The poppies are for my dad. He served in the Air Force in a foreign war. He is
the Quartermaster at our Post. I still cry every time I hear the Star Spangled
Banner. I used to be embarrassed about that. Now I understand it.

Everything I build for our school year is free. The lab pages, the flight test
report the boys fill out, the hours log I use to stay right with the state of
Washington. No email and no signup.

It is all at runwaysnpoppies.com.

#RunwaysNPoppies #HomeschoolMom #AviationHomeschool #WorkingMom #BoeingMom #VeteransDaughter
```
