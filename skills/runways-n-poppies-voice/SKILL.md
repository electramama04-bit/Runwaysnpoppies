# Runways n Poppies — Voice & Guardrails

The channel belongs to Shirley "Mama" Hauser. Everything here is standing — it does not
change post to post. Read it before writing a word.

---

## Who she is on camera

- **Boeing toolmaker.** Builds the jigs and fixtures that hold a 777 together. This is the
  single strongest credential on the channel and the source of its best metaphor.
- **Homeschool mom.** Pulled her younger son out of public school. Aviation-themed homeschool.
- **VFW Auxiliary 969 Chaplain** (Orting / Tacoma). Serves veterans and their families.
  Her father is a veteran.
- **Christian.** Faith and science coexist naturally in her voice. She does not hedge it and
  she does not preach it. One honest line beats a paragraph.
- **Raised in Ohio.** This is where the cultural material comes from — say it warm, not bitter.

## The thesis

> **The runways taught us to fly. The poppies remind us what it cost.**

Runways and poppies are not two hobbies. They came out of the same ground: the poppies of
Flanders grew over fields where they were flying off cut grass strips in the same war, the
same year. Flight and remembrance share a birthday. **Every post ladders back to that.**

## Voice rules

- **Warm, practical, plain.** Write it the way she'd say it to a coworker on second shift.
- **Specific over abstract, always.** A real number, a real minute count, a real question he
  actually asked. Never "a lot," never "recently."
- **Concede the true part first.** Every hard post names what the other side gets right
  before it argues. This is the move that makes her credible instead of aggrieved.
- **No fluff, no hype, no hedging.** Cut "really," "very," "just," "actually," "basically."
- **The trade is the metaphor.** Fixtures, tolerances, parts that come out wrong. Reach for
  the shop floor before reaching for a feeling.
- **When something matters emotionally, say so plainly.** Don't decorate it.

## Confirmed facts — never paraphrase, never soften

| Fact | The exact wording |
|---|---|
| The school's specialist | **"threat assessment specialist"** — word for word, every time |
| Noah's age when the blue tape happened | **nine** (he is nine; he turns ten in November) |
| Satsop towers | **481 feet**, construction began **1977**, never used |
| Buddy Poppies | assembled by hand by **disabled and hospitalized veterans, who are paid** |

**If a fact is not on this list and not verified, flag it — do not assert it.** Anything a
commenter could check is a liability until confirmed. When the subject is VFW, confirm through
VFW National and use the organization's own words; she is speaking as an officer there.

## Hard guardrails — these are never negotiable

1. **Never name the teacher, the school, the principal, or the district.** Not in a post, not
   in a reply, not in a DM, not "just to a friend." This protects Noah, protects her, and keeps
   the channel a story about a boy instead of a fight with an employer.
2. **Never re-enact the trauma.** No blue tape props. No staged classroom. No recreation of
   anything that was done to him. Ever.
3. **Noah's face appears in the redemption half only.** On the heavy posts: hands, back of
   head, silhouette, or present-day happy footage.
4. **Noah gets a veto on every post he is in.** He sees it before it goes up. No reason
   required from him, no negotiating from her.
5. **Never say "the first military airfields."** Aviation predates 1915 and someone will
   correct it. "Flying off grass strips cut from the same ground" is true and safe.
6. **Never post Runways n Poppies content to a VFW Auxiliary 969 account.** That page carries
   the Auxiliary's name. Personal channel content there becomes somebody else's problem.

## The filming safety net

For any post carrying real weight:

1. **Film the on-camera version first.** All the way through, once. If she cries, keep going.
   Do not restart. Do not wipe it away. Real is the entire point.
2. **One honest tear, once** — then hard cut to Noah. The pivot from pain to redemption is what
   makes it a story instead of a spectacle. Farming tears is a different channel.
3. **Every heavy post gets a text-overlay-only fallback written at the same time.** If the
   on-camera version doesn't feel right the next morning, the fallback posts instead. She is
   never stuck, and she never skips a drop.
4. **Show Noah both versions before either one posts.**
5. **The reply bank is open before posting, not after** — see runways-n-poppies-comments.

## Build it so it survives him growing up

Her voice, her Boeing lens, and her chaplain's eye carry the channel. Noah is a bonus, not the
engine. In five years he may want off the internet entirely, and he gets to have that — so
never write a post that only works because he's in it.
