# Runways n Poppies — Content Calendar

Four posts a week, four different formulas, so the feed never reads as formulaic and you
learn which formula your audience actually rewards.

The hard part is not the trip weeks. It is the other thirty-one.

## The week

| Day | Post | Formula | Length | Filmed |
|-----|------|---------|--------|--------|
| **Monday** | POV Reel | 2 | Under 15 sec | On the trip, or Sunday |
| **Wednesday** | Specific Outcome Reel | 1 or 7 | Under 30 sec | On the trip |
| **Friday** | Contrarian Reel | 3 | Under 30 sec | On the trip, or at the desk |
| **Sunday** | Facebook long-form | 8 cold open | About 3 min | The trip itself |

Sunday is the signature: a three-minute recap with a story arc, kid narration, one lesson,
and a warm outro. It is also the only slot where beautiful footage finally earns its place.

## Timing — a trip feeds the week AFTER it

This is the rule that breaks most plans, so it comes before everything else.

Most trips land on a Saturday or a weekend. Footage from a Saturday trip cannot fill the
Monday three days earlier. **A trip feeds the following week, not the week it happens.**

| Week | What posts | Where it comes from |
|------|-----------|---------------------|
| Trip week | Four posts | The bank, and desk-week content |
| Week after | Four posts | The trip |

So every Sunday session plans a week you **already have footage for**. If the plan depends
on footage you have not shot yet, it is not a plan, it is a hope.

Two consequences worth holding onto:

- **You cannot start this system from zero.** The first two weeks come out of the bank, so
  the bank gets built before the first post goes out. One Free First Thursday and one desk
  week is enough to start.
- **If the trip is on a Sunday, move the planning session to Friday.** Do not try to plan
  and travel on the same day.

## The arithmetic nobody plans for

There are **21 field trips** on the 2026-2027 calendar and **52 weeks** in the year. Most
weeks have no trip. A plan that assumes a trip every week collapses by October.

So the year runs on two things:

1. **The bank.** A good trip yields six to eight usable posts, not four. Post four, bank
   the rest. See "The bank" below.
2. **Desk-week content.** The Monday-to-Friday school blocks, the workbench, and the
   questions the boys ask are content. See `references/no-trip-weeks.md`.

## The Sunday planning session — thirty minutes, same order

1. **Check what footage you already have.** Last week's trip, the bank, anything filmed at
   the desk. This is what the coming week is built from. Yes goes to step 2. Nothing in
   hand goes to `references/no-trip-weeks.md`, then rejoin at step 3.
2. **Name the source and the day.** The location it came from, or the desk day it came
   from. Do not invent a trip to fit a hook, and do not plan against footage that does not
   exist yet.
3. **Pick four hooks** from **rnp-reel-hooks**, one per slot. Hooks first, before anything
   else is decided. The shot list serves the hooks, not the other way around.
4. **Check the rotation ledger.** Did any of these four formulas run last week? Swap it.
5. **Fill every "still needs."** Anything you cannot fill with a real fact, swap the hook.
6. **Assign the kid-fronted one.** At least one of four is Ethan or Noah.
7. **Pick the download each post points to.** One per post. If no download fits, that is a
   flag, not a shrug. See "The funnel" below.
8. **Check the bank.** Anything banked that fits this week better than what you just built?
9. **Write it on the planning sheet** in `references/planning-sheet.md` and log the four
   formulas in the ledger.
10. **Then look ahead.** Is there a trip in the coming week? If so, pick its four hooks now
    too and hand that sheet to **rnp-film-day**. That sheet is the shot list, and it feeds
    the week after next.

Each session therefore does two things: it finalizes the week you can film nothing more
for, and it sets the shot list for the trip ahead.

## The bank

A trip week produces more than a trip week needs. Bank the surplus instead of posting it
all at once and going quiet for three weeks.

- **Bank anything usable that did not make this week's four.** Extra takes of a hook, a
  second kid explanation, unused B-roll, a moment that did not fit the story.
- **Label each banked item with its formula and the download it points to**, so it drops
  into a future slot without rework.
- **Evergreen goes to the bank first.** A post about lift, or about the hours log, works in
  November as well as it works today. A post about a specific airshow does not.
- **Spend the bank on no-trip weeks**, not on lazy weeks. A banked post used the week after
  it was filmed is a wasted bank.
- **Anything sitting in the bank past about eight weeks either posts or gets cut.** Kids
  visibly age. Seasons change. Stale footage reads as stale.

Target: enter every trip week with three to five banked posts. That is the buffer that
keeps the feed alive through a six-week stretch with nothing on the calendar.

## The funnel — every post goes somewhere

Every post ends pointing at the free library on runwaysnpoppies.com. Not "follow along."
Not "link in bio," which is on the forbidden list in **vfw-969-social-captions**.

The site currently carries two downloads, both from the Flight & Friction unit:

| Download | Points from |
|---|---|
| The Four Forces, Pages 4-6 | Anything about lift, drag, thrust, weight, or a flight test |
| Flight Test Report, Day 10 | Anything about writing results down, assessment, or the annual review |

Two downloads cannot carry a year of posts. **When a post has no download that fits, that
is the signal to make one**, not a reason to drop the CTA. A post that lands well and sends
people to nothing is the most expensive kind of post there is.

### CTA bank

- "The lab sheet we used is free at runwaysnpoppies.com."
- "The flight test report the boys filled out is on the site. No email, no signup."
- "Comment PLANE and I'll send you the trip card for this one."
- "The whole Flight and Friction unit is free on the site."

Never "come learn with us" or "let's explore." Both are on the do-not-use list in
**rnp-reel-hooks** — they read as classroom marketing and get skipped.

## The rotation ledger

Keep a running log of which formula ran on which day. Two rules:

- **Never repeat a formula two posts running.**
- **Never repeat the same formula on the same weekday two weeks running.** Monday POV is
  the fixed exception, since POV is the slot.

Every eight weeks, look back. If one formula has run twice as often as the others, the
swipe file is being read lazily. Rotate deliberately.

## Reading the results, every two to three weeks

Open Facebook Insights and Instagram Insights and answer **one** question: which formula
held attention longest? Not which got the most likes. Which one **held**.

- Lean into the winner for a few weeks.
- Keep the rotation running underneath so you do not narrow to a single trick.
- Write the answer on the planning sheet so the next session starts from evidence.

Likes are a vanity number. Retention is the number that decides whether the next post gets
shown to anyone.

## Definition of done for a week

- [ ] Four hooks picked, all "still needs" filled
- [ ] Four formulas logged, none repeated from last week
- [ ] At least one post kid-fronted, both boys asked
- [ ] Every post has a download to point at
- [ ] Surplus banked and labeled
- [ ] Every post built from footage already in hand
- [ ] Next week's shot list handed to rnp-film-day if a trip is coming

## Open, needs Mama

- **Publishing path.** The VFW posts run through Airtable, n8n, and Blotato with hardcoded
  page IDs. Runways n Poppies needs its own Blotato account ID and Facebook page ID before
  **vfw-969-social-pipeline** can publish for this brand. Ask, do not guess.
- **Which platforms are live.** Facebook is confirmed. Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube
  handles need confirming before a post is written for them.
- **Calendar reconciliation.** The trip list in aviation-homeschool runs April 2026 to
  March 2027. The site's catalog labels 5th grade as starting September 8, 2026. If a
  planning question turns on which frame is current, ask rather than pick.

## What NOT to do

- Don't plan the week before picking the hooks.
- Don't plan a week around footage you have not shot yet.
- Don't invent a trip to fit a hook.
- Don't post the whole bank at once.
- Don't skip the CTA because no download fits. Make the download.
- Don't optimize on likes.
