# No-Trip Weeks — where content comes from the other thirty-one weeks

Twenty-one trips, fifty-two weeks. Most weeks have no field trip, and those weeks still
need four posts. Here is where they come from, in the order to reach for them.

## 1. Spend the bank first

Before building anything new, check what is banked. The bank exists exactly for this week.
If three of the four slots fill from the bank, this session takes ten minutes instead of
thirty.

## 2. The desk week is content

Noah's blocks run Monday to Friday, 9 to 11 AM. Every day has a post in it.

| Day | Subject | The post hiding in it |
|-----|---------|----------------------|
| Monday | Math Mammoth + reading | "My kid did math at the kitchen table and it was about airplanes." Numbers formula |
| Tuesday | Handwriting + One Question a Day Journal | Read the journal answer out loud. The kid's own words are the hook |
| Wednesday | Science | The experiment, filmed. Best download tie-in of the week |
| Thursday | History, aviation, paper airplanes | **The strongest desk-week day.** Paper airplane contest, aviation quiz, a story |
| Friday | Creative and engineering | Build something, film the build, show the failure first |

Thursday is the one to protect. Paper airplanes are visual, competitive, kid-fronted, and
tie straight to the Four Forces download. If a no-trip week gets only one filmed post, film
Thursday.

## 3. Free First Thursday, every month

Museum of Flight is free 5 to 9 PM the first Thursday of every month. That is a monthly
trip that is not on the 21-trip list. It costs nothing, it is thirty minutes away, and it
resets the bank. Treat it as a standing capture night.

## 4. The workbench and the tools

Boeing credibility without Boeing footage. Film at home.

- A tool on the bench, named by a kid in two seconds
- What a toolmaker actually does, explained to a 9-year-old
- The same tool at work and the same skill at the kitchen table
- 5S applied to a homeschool shelf, which is a genuinely good post

Nothing from inside a Boeing facility. Ever. See **rnp-film-day**.

## 5. The questions they ask

The single most renewable source you have. Keep a running note on your phone.

- "3 questions Noah asked this week that I couldn't answer"
- One question, one answer, one 20-second Reel
- Let them Google it on camera and film the finding

These cost nothing to produce and they are honest, which is why they hold.

## 6. The operations posts — for the homeschool parents watching

These convert better than trip posts for the audience most likely to download something.

- The hours log, and how 1,000 hours actually gets counted
- The Declaration of Intent, and the September 15 deadline
- What the 11 required subjects look like when the classroom is a runway
- RCW 28A.200 protects experiential teaching. Most parents do not know that
- What the memberships cost versus what they return

Every one of these points at a download. Several of them point at downloads that do not
exist yet, which is useful information.

## 7. The calendar gives you the theme

Aviation dates carry a post on their own, no trip required.

| Date | Post |
|------|------|
| Nov 2026 | National Aviation History Month, all month |
| Dec 17, 2026 | Wright Brothers Day |
| Jan 2027 | Epiphany and navigation, the Q4 theme |
| Feb 2027 | Winter engineering |
| May 20-21, 2027 | Lindbergh transatlantic 100th, the year's capstone |

Check the quarter theme in **aviation-homeschool** — Spring Flight, Summer Sky, Autumn
Wings, Winter Ascent. The theme is a content prompt.

## 8. Nature outings count

They are on the calendar, they are close, and they are cheap. Birds and flight is the same
unit. Merlin, eBird, the nature journal. Ethan running the app is a post.

## The no-trip week plan

| Slot | Where it comes from |
|------|---------------------|
| Monday POV | Bank, or the desk week |
| Wednesday outcome | An operations post. Hours, subjects, cost |
| Friday contrarian | An opinion. These need no footage beyond your face |
| Sunday long-form | Bank a trip recap, or film Thursday and build around it |

Friday is the slot that never needs a trip. A contrarian opinion filmed in the car on the
way to the grocery store is a complete post. When the week is thin, Friday is free.
