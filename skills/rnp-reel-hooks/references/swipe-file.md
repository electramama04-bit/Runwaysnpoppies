## Before any hook goes on camera

- Run the first three words alone. Do they pull? If not, cut filler off the front.
- Fill every bracket and every "still needs" item with something real. A generic fill
  kills the hook.
- Do not stack two hooks. One hook, one idea.
- No em-dashes in the caption body. No emojis in the body. Hashtags go in their own field.
- If a kid can deliver the line, the kid delivers the line.
