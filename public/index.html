How the year is built

Four quarters, each with its own weather and its own reason to look up.

Spring Flight

April through June. Easter, the space program, birds and the mechanics of wings, and the first pioneer pilots.

Summer Sky

July through September. Boeing's anniversary, the Blue Angels over Lake Washington, National Aviation Day, and the fall migration.

Autumn Wings

October through December. Forests and mushrooms, National Aviation History Month, Advent and the stars, and Wright Brothers Day.

Winter Ascent

January through March. Navigation, winter engineering, Lent, and the long build-up to the Lindbergh centennial.
